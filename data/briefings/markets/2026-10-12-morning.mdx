## Oil Eased on an Unconfirmed Russia-Ukraine Energy Truce, Even After a Deadly Weekend Strike in Riyadh

Brent is about 1% lower this morning, near $103-104, after President Trump said on Sunday that Russia and Ukraine had agreed to an "energy ceasefire" that neither side has confirmed. The weekend also brought the deadliest attack on Saudi Arabia in more than a decade and two more tankers hit near Hormuz, and oil fell anyway. That is the finding: the market gave more weight to a possible extra Russian supply than to new Gulf damage. The evidence cuts both ways. Ukraine is still waiting for details, and Russia struck a Kyiv power plant on Sunday, so the truce may not hold. Gulf flows have kept moving, which explains why the attacks moved prices less. If Brent climbs back above Friday's $104.7 settlement, the market has stopped trusting the truce.

## What changed since last report

Sunday night's report said a sustained move well above $105 would show the market treating the Oman-coast tanker route as at risk; Brent moved the other way, so the weekend attacks were absorbed for a second time.

- **Possible Russian supply.** The truce, and a separate arrangement to supply markets with Russian diesel, were read as [more Russian barrels reaching a short market](https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/12/stocks-rise-as-oil-prices-tumble-after-trump-hails-energy-ceasefire). Refineries are the target named in the deal, and refinery strikes are what had cut Russian fuel exports.
- **Gulf flows kept moving.** Reports cite [improved loadings through Hormuz and continued operations on Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline](https://hdfcsky.com/news/brent-crude-oil-price-today-october-12-2026-brent-falls-1-5percent-to-103-wti-drops-1-8percent-to-90-2-middle-east-risks-persist), the route that carries Saudi crude to Yanbu on the Red Sea. Attacks that do not stop barrels move the price less than attacks that do.
- **A softer dollar.** The dollar index slipped to [near 102.20](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/forex-today-us-dollar-softens-amid-hopes-russia-ukraine-energy-ceasefire-202610120654), which helped gold and European stocks.
- **No signal from rates.** The cash Treasury market is shut for Columbus Day, so the first reading on yields comes Tuesday.

## What to watch

**Brent against the truce.** Brent sits a few dollars above $100 and below Friday's $104.7. Confirmation from both Moscow and Kyiv, and a pause in refinery strikes that holds for several days, would support the move under $104. Further Russian strikes on Ukraine's grid, or a Houthi hit that stops Saudi exports, would take Brent back above $105. A second force sits under WTI: [Hurricane Isaias shut in about 72% of US Gulf of Mexico oil output last week](https://energynow.com/2026/10/hurricane-isaias-slams-u-s-gulf-coast-shutting-down-72-of-offshore-oil-production/), and how fast those barrels return affects US crude separately from the Gulf.
