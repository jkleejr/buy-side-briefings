### What it means

This section is interpretation, not reported fact. The weekend did not change either force behind this month's moves. Attacks on Saudi Arabia are continuing and diplomacy is still an unanswered offer, so oil reopens on Sunday evening from a risk premium that has grown three weeks running. Nothing over the weekend gives a reason for that premium to shrink, but nothing confirmed a new hit on Saudi export facilities either.

Bitcoin's break below $80,320 lasted only hours, and it finished the day slightly higher. A single dip on thin weekend trading says little about risk appetite on its own. It matters only if Monday's stock market extends Friday's losses.
