## Why it happened

This morning's report was right that money was rotating out of chips rather than out of stocks, and wrong that bank results would hold up the Dow, which fell 0.35% as Citigroup and Wells Fargo lost ground.

- **Chips carried two separate worries.** CXMT's design claim is a pricing threat to memory makers, while Hock Tan's casino comparison questions the spending that equipment makers depend on; [Applied Materials was among the weakest names](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/s-p-500-flat-as-chipmakers-fall-and-bank-earnings-roll-in-ce785dddd080f22c) and [ASML lost about 1.8% in Amsterdam](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/european-stocks-advance-despite-global-090614027.html) a day before it reports.
- **The largest names did not sell.** [Hyperscalers and Apple, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft and Nvidia offset the chip losses from the open](https://investrade.com/mid-morning-look-october-13-2026/), which is why the Nasdaq lost only 0.15% while the chip index fell 1.2%.
- **Banks were sold on costs, not profits.** All four large banks reported higher profits, but [the banks warned that rising rates and sticky inflation will bite](https://fortune.com/2026/10/13/jpmorgan-goldman-sachs-citigroup-wells-fargo-interest-inflation/), and [JPMorgan fell about 0.9% after a rare earnings miss](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/12/stock-market-today-live-updates.html). With the ten-year near 5.2%, credit provisions mattered more to investors than revenue beats.
- **Rates and oil eased together.** [Brent fell 96 cents to $105.69 as traders weighed more Russian diesel reaching the market](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/13/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-hormuz.html), still about $1 above the $104.7 line the Russia-Ukraine truce is judged against.
