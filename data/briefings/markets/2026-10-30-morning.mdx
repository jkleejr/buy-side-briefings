## Amazon and Intel Pull Nasdaq Futures Down About 2% While Bonds Rally

Nasdaq-100 futures are down 2.0%–2.5% before the open after Amazon fell about 7% and Intel about 25% premarket. That makes this the third day of selling over what AI spending costs. A 2% drop from Thursday's ~26,184 would take the Composite near 25,660. That is below Monday's 26,169.38 and below last Friday's 25,955.34 close, so the index would be down for the week. The selling is not spreading evenly. Dow futures are about flat, and Treasuries are rallying hard enough to cut into December hike pricing. The evidence cuts two ways. Amazon's problem is cash: it held its $220 billion capex plan and guided the holiday quarter below estimates. Intel's problem is PC demand. Those are two different reasons landing on the same index. The read no longer holds if the Nasdaq closes back above 26,169.38 today.

## What changed since last report

Last night's report said a close under 26,169.38 would erase the week's gain. Futures put the Nasdaq more than 500 points below that level, so the read held and has gone further than it set out.

- **Investors moved into Treasuries as tech fell, according to Reuters.** The 10-year fell to its lowest level in about a month, from a 5.365% high in early October, even as [Brent's expiring contract rose toward $116.59 after a tanker was hit in the Strait of Hormuz and Trump was briefed on options for new strikes on Iran](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40446647/oil-rises-on-supply-concerns-as-houthis-attack-saudi-arabia-list-3). - **The Bank of Japan held at 1.25%** as expected, and the decision did not slow the selling in Tokyo chip stocks.
