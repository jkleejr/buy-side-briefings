### What happened over the weekend

Saudi Arabia and Aramco had not confirmed any damage as of early Sunday.

- [Bitcoin was near $80,414 on Sunday morning, down 0.46% in 24 hours, after a low of $79,232 on Saturday and a high of $81,029 overnight](https://crypto.com/exchange/trade/BTC_USDT).

### What it means

This section is interpretation, not reported fact. Yanbu is the Red Sea port Saudi Arabia has used for most of its crude exports since the Strait of Hormuz closed in July. A claim against it goes to the one route that has kept Saudi oil moving. That is why the Sunday evening oil open matters more than usual.

The evidence cuts both ways. The Houthis have claimed many strikes this month, and Saudi Arabia has not confirmed damage from this round. If no damage is confirmed, Friday's $116.72 already prices a lot of risk. If Aramco reports an outage at Yanbu, that price may not be enough. This run cannot tell which, and neither can the market until Saudi officials or Aramco speak.

Bitcoin gives little signal. It is about 1% below Friday evening and has spent the weekend either side of the $80,320 October 8 low without a clear break. Weekend trading is thin.
