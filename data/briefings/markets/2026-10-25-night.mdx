## Oil Eases and Stock Futures Edge Up as a Week of Fed and Big Tech News Begins

Friday's closes are still the last full session. [The Dow fell 1.23% to 52,215.16, the S&P 500 0.52% to 7,623.68 and the Nasdaq 0.21% to 25,955.34](https://kiplinger.com/investing/stocks/dow-drops-652-points-as-oil-treasury-yields-rise-stock-market-today). [Brent settled at $116.72, its highest close since April 29, and the 10-year ended at 4.999%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/23/bonds-yields-us-treasurys.html).

### What happened at the Sunday open

- [Brent fell about 1.1% to around $115 and US crude about 1.2% to roughly $102 when oil reopened](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/25/stock-market-today-live-updates.html). Other price feeds put Brent between $115.70 and $115.90 later in the evening.
- [S&P 500 futures rose about 0.4%, Nasdaq-100 futures about 0.6% and Dow futures about 128 points, or 0.2%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/25/stock-market-today-live-updates.html).
- [Saudi Arabia and Aramco still had not confirmed damage from the Houthis' claimed weekend strikes on Yanbu, Rabigh and the East-West pipeline](https://houseofsaud.com/houthis-target-yanbu-aramco-samref/). The coalition reported only interceptions.
- [Bitcoin was near $80,158 on Sunday night, down 0.88% in 24 hours, with a 24-hour range of $79,835 to $81,029](https://crypto.com/exchange/trade/BTC_USDT).

### What it means

This section is interpretation, not reported fact. The morning report named Sunday's oil open as the first real test of the Yanbu claims. The test came back mild. Brent opened lower, not higher, and stayed well above the $113 level that would have shown the risk treated as contained. That reads as a market waiting for proof of damage rather than paying up for it.

The evidence still points both ways. A 1% dip leaves Brent about 20% above where it started October, so most of the month's risk premium is still in the price. The attacks on Saudi Arabia have not stopped, and an unconfirmed claim can become a confirmed outage overnight.

Stock futures are up, but a 0.4% move on a Sunday night is small next to Friday's losses and says little before Wednesday. Bitcoin is just below its $80,320 October 8 low again. It has crossed that level several times this weekend on thin trading, so this is not yet a clear break.

### What is on the calendar

- [The Fed decides on Wednesday, October 28. Fed funds futures put the chance of a hold at about 90%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/25/stock-market-today-live-updates.html). What the Fed says about oil and inflation matters more than the decision itself.
- [Microsoft and Alphabet report Wednesday after the close and Amazon on Thursday](https://www.babypips.com/analysis/fundies-cheat-sheet-2026-10-26-fed-big-tech-oil-saudi). Their spending plans are the next test of the AI selling that drove tech lower this month.
- September PCE inflation and a first read on third-quarter GDP are also due this week.

### What would change the picture

- Confirmed damage at Yanbu or on the East-West pipeline would likely push oil back above Friday's $116.72.
- Brent falling below $113 would show the market treating the Saudi attacks as contained.
- A 10-year above 5.11% with Brent above $113 before Wednesday would show oil-driven inflation setting rates.
- A 10-year back below 4.95% would show worry about growth and AI spending back in charge.
- A Nasdaq that stays below 26,424.82 into Wednesday's results would leave October's tech selling intact.
- A daily bitcoin close below $80,320 would turn the weekend's back-and-forth into a break.
