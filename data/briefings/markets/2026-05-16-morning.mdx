## Bear Case (counter-argument to the step-aside call)

Weekend crypto moves of -0.5% are within normal low-volume chop range and should not be read as confirmation of a regime change. Crypto routinely drifts on weekends; reading a single 0.5% overnight decline as "confirmation" is exactly the kind of pattern-completion bias that catches market participants at every short-term low.

The **absence of further panic over the weekend** — no -5% crypto crash, no DXY ripping higher, no gold catching a fear bid — is consistent with Friday being a localized flush rather than the start of a multi-week drawdown. Historically, meaningful market lows tend to form on Fridays when fear peaks; Monday's gap-down could mark exactly that low.

Position sizing on any short setups should also reflect that this call is built on limited new data since Friday's close. A regime call deserves more than 12 hours of weekend price action to confirm.

**Invalidation level:** if SPX reclaims 7,460 by midday Monday AND VIX prints below 17, the step-aside call is wrong. The right adjustment in that case is back to selective BUY by Tuesday's open.
