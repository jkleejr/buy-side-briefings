## Futures Point to a Pause in the AI Selling, Not a Reversal

Stock futures reopened Sunday evening a little higher. [CNBC reported Nasdaq-100 futures up 0.4% and S&P 500 and Dow futures up about 0.2%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/18/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), describing traders cautiously buying last week's dip in AI shares. [Bloomberg (subscription) reported larger gains of 0.5% and 0.9%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-18/stock-market-today-dow-s-p-live-updates). The two reports disagree on size but agree on direction.

The starting point is Friday. [The S&P 500 closed at 7,659.44, seven points under 7,666.45, while the Nasdaq lost 4.5% on the week and the Dow gained 0.7%](https://wtop.com/national/2026/10/how-major-us-stock-indexes-fared-friday-10-16-2026). That is the level recent reports named as the sign of selling spreading beyond AI. Sunday's futures gains would put the index back around that line, so Monday's close matters more than the overnight quote.

- [Iran's Revolutionary Guards said two supertankers hit mines and caught fire near Hormuz, and at least nine ships have been attacked around the strait since October 11](https://uk.investing.com/news/economy-news/iran-war-tankers-hit-near-hormuz-as-tehran-offers-us-reopening-plan-4915988). The supply risk behind Friday's oil price has not eased.
- [Brent settled Friday near $110, and Iran says the strait would reopen only if US forces leave the region](https://financefeeds.com/oil-price-near-110-as-iran-demands-us-withdrawal-but-keeps-hormuz-proposal-open/). A de-escalation that would bring oil down still depends on terms the US has not accepted.
- CNBC described oil as stable early Sunday. This run could not confirm a Brent or WTI price from the reopen.
- [Bitcoin traded near $82,455 on Sunday night, up 1.2% in 24 hours in an $80,665 to $82,470 range](https://crypto.com/exchange/trade/BTC_USDT). It is about $2,100 above its $80,320 low.

## What Would Change the Picture

**Stocks.** A Monday close back above 7,666.45 would make Friday look like an options-expiry overshoot. A second close below it with the Dow down more than 1% would show the selling spreading past AI.

**Oil.** A Brent settle above $110 would add to the inflation pressure that keeps a Fed rate rise in discussion before the October 27-28 meeting. A fall back toward $104.7 would ease it.

**Earnings.** [Netflix reports Tuesday and Tesla, Meta and Texas Instruments report Wednesday](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/16/major-earnings-reports-market-fears-next-week.html). Meta's spending plans are the first direct read from a large AI buyer since the chip selling began. Spending held or raised would argue against the slowdown chip prices imply, and a cut would support it.

## What Is Uncertain

Overnight futures trade on thin volume, and gains this small often fade by the close. The weekend tanker reports are disputed: Iran says mines, while US Central Command has said it cleared the mines and ships are being hit by projectiles. The Fed is in its pre-meeting quiet period, so no officials will speak to rates before October 27-28.
