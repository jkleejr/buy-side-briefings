## Why it happened

The morning report's read was mixed. It was right that stocks would rise with bonds and that a record close was within reach. It was wrong on two points it had flagged: oil's pre-market decline reversed into a flat settlement, and the thirty-year did not close back under 5.644%.

- **The intermediate curve rallied; the long end did not.** The ten-year fell about 5 basis points and the three-year auction cleared close to average, which is what equity valuations key off. The thirty-year was flat, widening its premium over the ten-year to about 39 basis points from about 35 at Monday's close, so the pressure on the longest bonds that began with [Monday's 74.0 ISM services prices reading](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/services-pmi-at-54-9-september-2026-ism-services-pmi-report-302898421.html) has not eased. A steeper curve on a day with no new inflation data points toward investors asking more to hold long duration ahead of Thursday's sale.
- **Oil stopped falling because supply around Hormuz is still under attack.** [Gulf exports have recovered while attacks on tankers near the strait have increased](https://cnbc.com/2026/10/06/crude-oil-tanker-strait-hormuz-iran-attack.html), and the US strategic reserve stands at 283 million barrels, its lowest since 1982. The input behind Monday's long-bond selling is unchanged on the day.
- **Europe eased rather than added.** [European stocks rose for a third session as French debt markets calmed](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/european-stocks-tiptoe-higher-as-french-debt-calms-ahead-of-retail-sales-4933417), and the German ten-year fell about 6 basis points to 3.454% in the morning. The euro-driven pressure that lifted the dollar above 102 on Monday did not intensify, so Tuesday's US rally had help from abroad that Thursday's sale may not.
- **The record came from AI spending, not from rates broadly.** [Google agreed a 20-year deal for 3,590 megawatts from 11 Constellation reactors](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-10-06-Google-and-Constellation-Announce-Landmark-Agreement-to-Bring-890-MW-of-New-Nuclear-Capacity-to-PJM-Grid-as-Part-of-Long-Term-Power-Deal), Marvell raised its outlook, and [Nvidia moved closer to a $6 trillion market value](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/nvidia-heads-6-trillion-value-084336187.html). Small companies, which depend more on borrowing costs and domestic demand, fell.
