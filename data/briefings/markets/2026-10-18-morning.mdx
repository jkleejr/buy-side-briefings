> **Methodology note:** Sunday morning report. No stock, bond or oil market has traded since Friday's close; bitcoin is the only live price. Sources cited inline. This run could check most sources only through search results, not by reading the articles in full, so conviction is low.

## The Weekend Added Oil Risk, Not New Information About the AI Selling

Nothing has traded since Friday except bitcoin. [The S&P 500 closed Friday at 7,659.44, seven points under 7,666.45, while the Nasdaq lost 4.5% on the week and the Dow gained 0.7%](https://wtop.com/national/2026/10/how-major-us-stock-indexes-fared-friday-10-16-2026). That is the level recent reports named as the sign of selling spreading beyond AI. The break is narrow and came on monthly options-expiry day, so one close does not confirm it.

The weekend news was about oil. [Iran's Revolutionary Guards said two tankers carrying about 2 million barrels of crude each caught fire after hitting mines in Hormuz](https://gcaptain.com/irans-guards-say-two-tankers-hit-mines-in-strait-of-hormuz/). [US Central Command says it cleared the strait of mines and that ships are being hit by projectiles, and both sides claim control of the strait](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/17/us-and-iran-both-claim-control-of-strait-of-hormuz-amid-attacks-on-vessels). Neither account has been independently confirmed. They agree that ships are still being struck. Brent settled Friday at $109.86, before these reports, so oil's Sunday evening open is the first price on them.

- [Attacks on tankers in Hormuz reached their highest of any week since the war began in February](https://gcaptain.com/attacks-on-tankers-in-hormuz-hit-highest-of-any-week-since-start-of-iran-war-sources-say/). The supply risk behind Friday's oil gain has not eased over the weekend.
- [UKMTO logged 12 attacks on ships in the week to October 16 and says most attacks go unreported](https://gcaptain.com/fog-of-war-deepens-in-hormuz-as-ukmto-says-most-ship-attacks-go-unreported/). The true count is likely higher than the reported one, which makes the oil risk hard to size.
- [The chip index fell for an eighth straight day on Friday and 11.4% on the week, its longest losing run since 2008](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/16/investing/tech-stocks-ai-coreweave). The weekend brought no new information on the AI financing worries behind it.
- [The two-year yield fell about 3 basis points and FedWatch put an October hold above 80%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/16/us-treasury-yields-investors-hot-inflation-data-.html). Rate risk eased while chips fell, so interest rates are not what drove the week.
- [Bitcoin traded near $81,923 on Sunday morning, up 0.5% in 24 hours in an $80,817 to $82,295 range](https://crypto.com/exchange/trade/BTC_USDT). It is about $1,600 above its $80,320 low and has not joined the stock selling.

## What Would Change the Picture

**Oil.** A Brent settle above $110 would add to the inflation pressure that keeps a Fed hike in play before the October 27-28 meeting. A fall back toward $104.7 would ease it. The two accounts of how ships were hit matter here: mines would be slower to clear than projectile attacks.

**Stocks.** A close back above 7,666.45 early in the week would make Friday look like an options-expiry overshoot. A second close below it with the Dow down more than 1% would show selling spreading past AI.

**Earnings.** [Netflix reports Tuesday and Tesla, Meta and Texas Instruments report Wednesday](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/16/major-earnings-reports-market-fears-next-week.html). Meta's capital spending plans are the first direct read from a large AI buyer since the chip selling began. Spending held or raised would argue against the slowdown chip prices imply, and a cut would support it.

## What Is Uncertain

The cause and scale of the weekend tanker incidents are disputed and unverified. Analysts are split on the AI selling: Jefferies called the CoreWeave selloff overdone, while others expect risk aversion to dominate the coming weeks. The Fed is in its pre-meeting quiet period, so no officials will speak to rates before October 27-28.
