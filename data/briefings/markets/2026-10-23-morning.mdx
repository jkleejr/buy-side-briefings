## What changed since last report

Last night's report set the test as the 10-year below 5.0% with Brent above $113; both still hold, but the gap between them narrowed overnight.

- **The oil move is about Saudi infrastructure, not Iran's exports.** The strikes and the Aramco evacuation came a day after the US State Department ordered diplomats' families out of Saudi Arabia, and [reports say a second US aircraft carrier is deploying](https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/Oil-Prices-Head-for-Weekly-Gain-as-Hormuz-Attacks-Rattle-Markets.html). That puts Saudi supply, not only Hormuz, inside the risk premium.
- **Bonds stopped rallying once oil kept rising.** Thursday's fall in yields came from money leaving tech, not from softer data: claims were 194,000. With futures stable, that demand eased and the 10-year edged up with oil.
- **The tech selling did not carry into Asia.** The Kospi's 2.7-percentage-point swing from its open, with SK hynix up about 4.4% after it suspended a Solidigm IPO, shows buyers stepped in at Thursday's lows. US chip futures have not confirmed that yet.

## Movers
