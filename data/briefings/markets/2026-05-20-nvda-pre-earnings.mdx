## What's Priced In

The street consensus at [$78.8B implies ~79% YoY revenue growth](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/2026/05/nvidia-earnings-preview-q1-2027) — an extraordinary number — but the bar is high because NVDA guided $78.0B itself in February. A $78B print is an in-line, not a beat. The market needs $79B+ to score it a beat; buy-side whispers are in the $80–81B range.

**The more important number is Q2 guidance.** Street consensus sits at [$86.08B, +9.3% sequential growth](https://www.techi.com/nvda-earnings-may-20-q2-guide-q1-beat/). Whisper is $90B. The activation levels:
- **≥$88B:** Bull acceleration narrative re-engages; above-consensus by $2B required for meaningful upside
- **$85–87B:** In-line; stock digests; no incremental story
- **<$85B:** Bear narrative activates; "peak AI capex" thesis runs

**Gross margin is the quiet watch item.** NVDA guided [75% ±50bps; consensus is 74.5%](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/261913674-nvidia-nvda-earnings-blackwell-datacenter-guidance-gross-margin-ai-infrastructure-valuation-tradingkey). Blackwell NVL configurations carry elevated CoGs during ramp. A sub-74% GM print is a structural story that overrides a revenue beat.

**China remains zeroed out.** The [April 2025 H20 export ban produced a $5.5B charge](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/blog/nvidia-q1-fy27-earnings-preview-may-20-2026/) and NVDA's Q1 guidance assumed no DC compute revenue from China. H200 re-engagement with ByteDance, Alibaba, and Tencent is approved under a 15% US revenue-share, but no material revenue has been booked. Any incremental China path is upside to numbers; any negative commentary is already in guidance.

## Bear Case (counter-argument to being long into the print)

Counter-argument: NVDA enters this print up approximately 78% from its April lows and trading at 33× forward earnings — a valuation that has no margin for a guide miss. The 1.7% earnings-day bid and [positive call skew](https://averin.com/en/journal/nvda-earnings-may-2026-options-iv-strategy) signal institutional pre-positioning, not fresh discovery. When smart money has already expressed the bull view pre-print, a beat without surprise *magnitude* leaves no incremental buyer; the stock stalls or reverses into the IV crush. A Q2 guide at $85B — technically sequential growth — but below the $87B the bull narrative requires could produce -6% on a nominal beat: a textbook sell-the-news outcome. The $5.5B H20 charge and permanent China exclusion are structural impairments that the bull thesis requires hyperscaler demand to fully offset; if Microsoft or Meta shifts even 5% of capex to custom silicon, the gap closes faster than the consensus model assumes.

**Invalidation:** Q2 guidance ≥$88B AND incrementally positive China commentary. If both conditions are met, cover any hedges at Thursday's open — the bull case is confirmed.
