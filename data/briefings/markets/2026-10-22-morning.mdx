## Doubts About AI Spending Return as Nasdaq Futures Fall Nearly 2% After Lam, Tesla and Meta

Nasdaq 100 futures were down 1.7% to 1.9% and S&P 500 futures about 1%, which would hand back all of Wednesday's 1.05% Nasdaq gain. The three results that moved prices each touched the cost of AI building from a different side. Lam Research's guide only matched estimates after a near-80% gain in a year. Tesla cut its 2026 spending floor to $20 billion from $25 billion and fell 3% to 5%. Meta beat on revenue but created a new non-voting share class that gives it a way to fund its buildout with stock, and slipped about 2%. Seoul and Tokyo sold chipmakers hard overnight. Wednesday's buying of reported AI revenue was real, and Super Micro's 27% raise still stands, but one strong session did not settle the funding question. A Nasdaq close below Tuesday's 26,424.82 would mean Wednesday's rebound failed.

## What changed since last report

Last night's report said a Nasdaq close below 26,424.82 would show the rebound rested on two companies' results; futures now imply an open near that line, about 1% below Wednesday's close.

- **The question moved from revenue to funding.** Wednesday paid for revenue already reported. Overnight results raised how the building gets paid for: Meta with a share class, Tesla by spending less. Both fell, so the market is marking down spending cuts and new funding alike.
- **Oil rose and bond yields fell together.** [Brent gained 3.2% to $112.27](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-trump-us-ceasefire-talks-ongoing/) after Iran's Revolutionary Guard said seven tankers were hit overnight, a claim no shipper or government had confirmed. The 10-year still fell about 9 basis points. Last night's report expected a Brent settle above $111.99 to push the yield toward 5.331%; this morning buyers are treating Treasuries as a refuge from stocks instead, so oil is not setting yields today.
- **The selling started in Asia.** Seoul and Tokyo chipmakers fell 4% to 5.5% before Europe opened, and one regional report tied it to doubts that AI building holds up in 2027.
