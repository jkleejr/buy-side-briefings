## Chip Stocks Lead a Rebound After Anthropic Files for Its IPO, Two Days Before Big Tech Reports

Anthropic's public IPO filing gave the AI trade its first good day in a week, and chips did most of the work. The S&P 500 rose 0.70% and the Nasdaq 0.82%, while the semiconductor fund gained 2.85%, about four times the index. The filing puts a public price on a frontier AI lab for the first time and shows its revenue growing fast enough to justify the compute it has committed to buy, which answers part of the funding question Elon Musk raised this morning. The evidence cuts both ways: the same filing shows an $80.7 billion first-half loss and a quarter of revenue from two customers, and the Nasdaq is still below last week's 26,424.82 level. A Nasdaq close above 26,424.82 after Microsoft and Alphabet report on Wednesday would show October's tech selling ending; a close back under Friday's 25,955 would show today was a one-day bounce.

- [Anthropic filed its S-1 to list Class A shares on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley leading](https://www.anthropic.com/news/s1-sec) — the share count and price range are not set, and the listing could come as soon as mid-November.
- [The S-1 shows $16.3 billion of first-half revenue against $4.6 billion for all of 2025, with about 25% from two unnamed customers](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/anthropic-s-1-shows-163b-h1-revenue-25-from-two-customers-and-807b-non-cash) — growth strong enough to carry a reported $518 billion of compute commitments, and concentrated enough that two contracts matter.
- [The S&P 500 closed up 0.70% at 7,676.77, the Nasdaq up 0.82% at 26,169.38 and the Dow up 0.28%](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-oct-26-2026) — the Dow, Friday's laggard, gained least, so today's buying went back into tech rather than broadly.
- [Nvidia rose 2.4%, AMD 2.8%, Micron 3% and Broadcom 2.3%, while the semiconductor fund gained nearly 3%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) — the companies that sell the compute rose most, which is where a $518 billion buyer shows up.
- [Fed funds futures still put a December hike at about 79%, with a hold on Wednesday at about 90%](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/interest-rates/cme-fedwatch-tool.html) — the rate backdrop that drove October's tech selling has not changed.

## Why it happened

This morning's report expected the session to turn on oil and the 10-year; the result was mixed. The 10-year did stay under 5% at 4.986%, as the morning set out, but oil's fall was smaller than the early quotes suggested, and the day's driver was Anthropic, which the morning did not have.

- **The filing answered a funding question.** Musk's warning that debt markets have nearly shut for AI data-center companies pointed at the buyers of chips. A listing that could [value Anthropic above $2 trillion](https://www.forbes.com/sites/fionariley/2026/10/26/anthropic-files-for-ipo-heres-what-we-learned/) opens equity markets as a funding source for the largest of those buyers, which is why the chip suppliers rose more than the software names.
- **Not all of big tech rose.** Alphabet, which competes with Anthropic and holds a stake in it, fell about 1.1% while Microsoft rose about 1.5%. The market treated the filing as good for compute suppliers, not for every AI company.
- **Rates stayed out of the way.** The 10-year traded between 4.957% and 5.005% and [closed at 4.986%](https://www.investing.com/rates-bonds/u.s.-10-year-bond-yield-historical-data), and the VIX fell to 18.49. With the Fed's two-day meeting starting Tuesday, bonds did not add to or take from the move.

## Movers

**Brazil** had its worst day since March 2020: [the Ibovespa fell 13.88% and the Brazil ETF closed down 19.8% at $34.15](https://www.riotimesonline.com/brazil-stocks-close-down-13-9-percent-lula-win-2026/) after Lula won 51.79% of the runoff, an outcome markets had priced at about 20%. The ETF recovered from an intraday fall of about 26%, and the dollar closed near R$5.41 after touching R$5.60; a return to that high would show the selling resuming rather than settling.

**Tesla** rose [5.21% after Barclays upgraded it to overweight and raised its target to $450 from $370](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9143012/tesla-tsla-shares-rise-as-barclays-boosts-price-target-to-450), reaching its highest level since July. The stock is still down about 13.7% for the year, so the move recovers part of a weak year rather than extending a strong one.

**Oil** eased on Iran diplomacy, with Saudi Arabia's defence minister in Tehran, but [CNBC put Brent's settlement at $115.81, down only 0.8%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/oil-prices-today-wti-brent.html), while other feeds showed it near $111. If the higher figure is right, the morning's drop toward $110 mostly reversed, and Friday's $116.72 remains close.

## What to watch

**The Nasdaq between 25,955.34 and 26,424.82 through Amazon on Thursday.** Today recovered a little under half the distance, and [an RBC strategist still warned of a possible 5% to 10% S&P 500 pullback in the near term](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/25/stock-market-today-live-updates.html). Microsoft and Alphabet report Wednesday after the Fed decision and Amazon on Thursday. Spending plans held or raised would confirm that the large buyers are still paying for compute, and a close above 26,424.82 would show October's tech selling ending. A cut in capital spending, or a close back under 25,955, would show today as a bounce on a single headline.

**The 10-year against 5% and 5.11% into Wednesday at 2pm.** A hold is priced at about 90%, so the statement and Chair Warsh's press conference on the roughly 79% December hike odds are what can move bonds. Pushback on December would likely take the 10-year below 4.95%. A statement tying further hikes to oil and the price pressure in last week's PMI would test 5.11%, which is what pulled tech lower earlier in the month.

**Brent against $116.72 and Bitcoin against $80,320.** Diplomacy with Iran is under way, but Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia have not stopped, and Brent above Friday's $116.72 would put oil back in charge of rates. Bitcoin has traded between $79,342 and $80,838 over 24 hours, on both sides of its $80,320 October 8 low; it did not join today's tech rebound, and a daily close below that low would turn weeks of crossing it into a break.
