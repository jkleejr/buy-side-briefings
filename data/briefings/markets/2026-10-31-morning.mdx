## What changed since last report

Last night's report said its read would no longer hold if the Nasdaq closed back above 25,955.34; nothing has traded to test that. What the weekend changed:

- **No new tanker incident.** The mine claims that circulated Saturday date to Thursday, October 29: [Iran's navy said four tankers struck mines, and the US military has said the main transit lanes were cleared](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-donald-trump-qatar-mediators-new-proposal-end-war/). An earlier version of this report treated them as weekend news. Friday's oil settle already included them.
- **Flows were already falling.** [Hormuz crude flows dropped to 7.3 million barrels a day in the week to Wednesday, more than 40% below the prewar level, and at least 30 vessels were hit in October, double September's 15](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/30/tanker-attack-strait-hormuz-iran-war-oil.html). That is the supply picture any post-election strike would start from.
- **Binance's hole is mostly filled.** [Binance said it has covered up to 90% of the roughly $6.3 billion stolen and plans to resume withdrawals Sunday, subject to security checks](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/31/binance-hack-asset-coverage-withdrawals.html). Bitcoin rose 0.9% and stayed above Friday's $76,176 low.
- **The inflation backdrop firmed.** [Treasury set the new I bond rate at 5.51%, with a 1.40% fixed portion that is the highest since 2006; September CPI ran 3.7%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/30/treasury-i-bond-rate-through-april-2027.html). That helps explain why [December hike odds fell only to 72.7% from 83.0%](https://www.actionforex.com/action-insight/market-overview/659298-dollar-falls-across-the-board-as-markets-scale-back-fed-hike-expectations-beyond-december/) during Friday's bond rally.

## Movers

**Intel** fell 29% to about $65 on Friday, and on Saturday [Bank of America, Roth and Seaport cut it to neutral while New Street upgraded it to buy](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/31/intel-not-a-buy-after-drop.html). Its data center and AI revenue rose nearly 80% to $7.3 billion, so the miss came from PCs and production limits rather than AI demand. A Monday close below Friday's $65.76 would mean the downgrades are still being priced in.

**Apple** reports Monday after the close, and [Bank of America forecasts a much weaker December quarter](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/10/30/bank-of-america-forecasts-a-much-weaker-december-quarter-for-apple.html). It is the first large tech report since the selling moved from AI buyers to chipmakers, and it tests whether tech outside that trade is spared.
