## Alphabet Falls After Hours on Cash Burn While Microsoft Rises, After a Fed Hold With Two Hike Dissents

Microsoft and Alphabet both reported cloud growth well above forecasts tonight, and the market split them on cash. Azure grew 48% while Microsoft spent about $5 billion less than expected, and its shares rose 2.5%–4% after hours. Google Cloud grew 103%, but Alphabet's $51.4 billion of capex left free cash flow at negative $9.07 billion, and its shares fell 8%–10%. Demand for AI computing is not in question; the market is pricing what it costs to supply. That leaves the chip suppliers in the middle: Alphabet's planned $300 billion of 2027 spending is their revenue, but investors are selling the stock of the company committing to it. The read no longer holds if Amazon's results on Thursday reward higher spending.

- [Alphabet's third-quarter capex was $51.4 billion and free cash flow negative $9.07 billion, its second straight negative quarter, and it guided to a significant increase in 2027 spending](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/alphabet-googl-earnings-q3-2026.html) — the shares fell about 10% after hours from a $322.22 close, with EPS of $2.12 against about $3.02 expected, much of the gap an $8.7 billion paper loss on equity stakes.
- [Azure revenue rose 48% to about $33.2 billion, above Microsoft's own 44%–45% constant-currency guide](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/microsoft-azure-revenue-surges-48-to-33-2b-beating-expectations-as-cloud-and-ai-demand-grows/) — Microsoft showed more cloud growth for less spending than forecast, and the market rewarded that combination.
- [The Fed held at 3.75%–4.00% in a 10-2 vote, with Governor Cook and Cleveland's Hammack dissenting for a hike, and called growth "strong" rather than "solid"](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/fed-meeting-today-live-updates.html) — a hold with two hawkish dissents keeps December open.
- [December hike odds rose to 85.7% on CME FedWatch from about 76.5% this morning, and the 10-year rose about 3 bp to near 5.02% while the 2-year fell to 4.704%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/us-treasurys-bonds-yields-federal-reserve.html) — long yields rose as short yields fell, so the bond market is pricing inflation risk more than a single hike.
- [The semiconductor index rose 1.75% to 11,684.00 while the S&P 500 added 0.28% to 7,743.98](https://cappnotes.substack.com/p/closing-look-wednesday-october-28) — SK Hynix's record quarter carried chips during the session, before the after-hours results.

## Why it happened

This morning's report said higher spending with cloud growth to match would support the chip demand story. That read was mixed: both companies showed the cloud growth, but the market rewarded the one that spent less. The Fed read held as written, with a hold and December left open, and the Nasdaq closed above 26,424.82.

- **Cash, not demand, separated the two.** [Microsoft's capex came in near $45.2 billion against a $50.67 billion estimate](https://pro.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/2026-10-28) (TheStreet, subscription), alongside adjusted EPS of $4.89 against $4.73 and revenue of $92.28 billion against $90.69 billion. Alphabet's ran to $51.4 billion and turned free cash flow negative again, against expectations of roughly breakeven. Before tonight, [hyperscaler stocks had fallen about a quarter since May](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/microsoft-reports-october-28-hyperscalers-132026963.html) on the same funding question that drove Tuesday's supplier-financing deals.
- **The Fed added to inflation worry, not to near-term hikes.** The two dissents and the "strong" growth language lifted December odds, yet the 2-year fell. At the press conference [Warsh said there were "no firm conclusions" on whether September's hike is working](https://www.federalreserve.gov/mediacenter/files/FOMCpresconf20261028.pdf), which leaves the decision to the data, starting with Thursday's PCE. A 10-year at 5.02% with oil rising says the bond market expects inflation to outlast one hike.
- **Oil rose on Iran.** [WTI rose 2.9% to $102.78 on reports the US may renew strikes on Iran after the midterms](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/oil-price-today-wti-brent-hormuz.html), which took back all of Tuesday's drop. The rise in long yields weighed on utilities and real estate.
