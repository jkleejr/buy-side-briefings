**SPCX's bond-offering shock.** [SpaceX filed its inaugural $20 billion bond offering (senior unsecured notes, proceeds earmarked to repay the bridge loan used to acquire xAI in February 2026) on June 22](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318874/20260622/spacex-stock-falls-16-20-billion-bond-debut-capital-already-owed.htm). **AI hardware rotation: MU +8.70%, TSM +6.9%.** In sharp contrast to the AI software sell-off, the AI hardware complex staged a pre-earnings rally. [MU surged +8.70% to $1,133.99 on analyst price-target upgrades (TD Cowen $1,500; RBC $1,200; Aletheia $1,600; Bernstein Buy reaffirm)](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/261975340-mu-q3-earnings-preview-june-2026-tradingkey), and TSM gained +6.9% to $462.12 on broader semiconductor rotation. **The Great Rotation: Russell 2000 +2.12% vs Nasdaq -1.32%.** The 3.44 percentage-point spread between the Russell 2000 and Nasdaq is the widest of the post-FOMC recovery.
