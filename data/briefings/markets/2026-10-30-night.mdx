## Intel's Worst Day Since 1974 Takes Chip Stocks Down About 7% and Gives the Nasdaq Its Worst Month Since March 2025

Intel fell 28.58% to $65.76, its largest one-day drop since 1974. The selling spread across the chip sector, which fell about 7% and lost roughly $1 trillion of market value. The Nasdaq fell 2.86% to 25,437.00, so it finished the week about 2% lower and October down 5.3%. On Friday the selling moved from the companies buying AI hardware to the companies making it. Amazon recovered most of a 7% premarket loss and closed about 2% lower. Bonds rallied and December hike odds fell, but oil rose 2.4% to a six-month high, so lower yields came with a higher inflation risk. The read no longer holds if the Nasdaq closes back above 25,955.34.

## Why it happened

This morning's report said the Nasdaq would finish the week lower unless it got back above 26,169.38. It closed at 25,437.00, below last Friday's 25,955.34, so the direction was right. The cause was wrong. The report expected Amazon's cash outflow to lead the selling, but Amazon recovered and chips led the decline.

- **Intel's miss was priced as a sector problem.** [Intel cut its fourth-quarter revenue outlook by about $300 million and expects a similar hit in the first quarter](https://techcentral.co.za/intel-shares-tumble-supply-constraints-cash-burn/287845/), and Reuters reported it will burn cash this year. Most of that is specific to Intel: PC demand, its own factories and the Israel plant. The European tech index also [fell 2.75%, its worst day since July 7, with ASML down 5%](https://lufkindailynews.com/news_reuters/business/european-shares-stumble-at-end-of-best-month-since-june-on-ai-worries/article_d239e095-9e46-5467-8b7b-49b70e61b1ae.html). That shows the selling was about the chip group, not only Intel.
- **Cash, not demand, is still the concern.** [Amazon's free cash flow was negative $42.4 billion over 12 months against positive $14.8 billion a year earlier](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/30/amazon-earnings-analysts-stock.html), yet the stock recovered from its premarket low. A spender that held up while its suppliers fell suggests the market is now asking whether spending will slow, which would hit chip revenue first.
- **Bonds rallied and gold fell together.** [The 10-year fell to about 4.91%, roughly 40 bp below its September 30 close of 5.293%](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/10/30/us-treasury-yields-fed-interest-rates.html). Even so, [gold broke below $3,900 to a one-year low](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/breaking-gold-crashes-below-3-900-eyes-on-ytd-low-202610301545). Lower yields and lower hike odds usually help gold, so the rate explanation does not account for that move.

## Movers

**Reddit** fell 29.22% to $91.73, its worst day since its 2024 listing. It [guided fourth-quarter revenue to $1.0–$1.03 billion against $1.05 billion expected, and said search referrals fell as Google expands AI Overviews](https://deadline.com/2026/10/reddit-shares-plunge-q3-search-traffic-earnings-1237209538/). That puts a number on how Google's AI search is costing sites that depend on its traffic.

**AMD** fell 10.88% to $504.80, more than twice Nvidia's 4.22% decline. It reports Tuesday after the close, so it is the first chipmaker to answer Intel's guide with its own numbers.

**Coinbase** fell after [Binance's $6.3 billion theft triggered $1.57 billion of crypto liquidations](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/30/crypto-liquidations-jump-to-usd1-57-billion-as-binance-hack-sends-bitcoin-ether-lower). Reports put its decline anywhere from 4.8% to 13%, depending on when they were written. Bitcoin itself ended the US day roughly flat over 24 hours.

## What to watch

**Chips into AMD on Tuesday.** The semiconductor index has now had two drops of more than 6% in just over a week. AMD at $504.80 and Nvidia at $204.64 are the closes to measure against. A guide from AMD that holds up would make Intel's miss look specific to Intel. A cautious one would support the view that AI spending is starting to slow. On the Nasdaq, a close back above 25,955.34 undoes the week's decline; a close under 25,437 extends it.

**Oil against bonds into the jobs report.** Brent's January contract at $113.43 is now the front month. The 10-year at about 4.91% has fallen on the tech selling while oil rose 13.6% in October. A 10-year back above 5.11% before Friday would mean oil's inflation risk has taken over from the demand for safety. December odds holding near 72.7% would say the hike is still expected.

**Bitcoin between $76,176 and $80,320.** BTC is at $77,173, about $1,000 above Friday's low and under the October 8 low. Monthly options have expired, and the 18,270 BTC traced to the hacker's wallets remains a possible source of selling. A daily close under $76,176 would extend the decline. A move back above $80,320 would make the theft a contained shock.
