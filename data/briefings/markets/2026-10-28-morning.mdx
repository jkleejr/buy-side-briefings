## What changed since last report

Last night's report said the Nasdaq had cleared 26,424.82 on a narrow, chip-led rally carried by supplier financing. The rally has held overnight, and two things have changed beneath it.

- **The demand case got a number.** SK Hynix beat consensus by about 6 trillion won and said supply stays short through 2030. Asia took that as confirmation: [the Kospi rose 6.8% and Taiwan's Taiex 1.8%, while Hong Kong fell 0.7%](https://www.ksat.com/business/2026/10/28/shares-in-asia-rise-after-a-rally-on-wall-street-and-oil-prices-gain-more-than-1/). That makes this a sector move rather than a broad one.
- **Oil has turned back up, and rates followed.** [Brent rose 0.87% to $114.87 and WTI 1.59% to $101.49 after Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia and US threats to renew strikes on Iran](https://thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/10/28/oil-prices-up-due-to-houthi-strikes-and-us-threat-to-renew-iran-war). That retraces most of Tuesday's 1.7% drop. The 10-year moved back above 5% with it, and the dollar is near a two-week high at 102.42.
- **Today's decision is about December, not today.** A hold is about 91% priced, so the statement and Chair Warsh's 2:30pm press conference move markets only through what they say about December.

Levels for today: the Nasdaq against 26,424.82, the 10-year against 5.11%, and Brent against Friday's $116.72.
