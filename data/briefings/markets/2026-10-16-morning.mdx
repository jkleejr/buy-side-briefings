## What changed since last report

Last night's report named Warsh as the test of whether the rotation out of chips could hold if rates rose. Rates did not rise, so that test did not happen; what changed is that the rates explanation for the AI selling lost another piece of support.

- **Warsh removed the near-term hike risk without removing the hike.** He declined to commit to any meeting, and Goldman Sachs read his remarks alongside other officials' as pointing to an October pause and a December increase. That is why October odds fell by about 10 points while the ten-year moved about 3 basis points.
- **Asia bounced after a steep fall, not on new information about AI demand.** Seoul analysts called the sell-off excessive and pointed to weak buying support rather than lower earnings forecasts, and Samsung led the gain rather than SK hynix.
- **The dollar fell and gold rose.** Spot gold at $4,176.70 is up $6.90 but [down about 0.5% on the week](https://brecorder.com/news/40444523/gold-gains-on-softer-dollar-but-heads-for-weekly-fall), with [$4,200 cited as the next resistance](https://www.fxleaders.com/news/2026/10/16/gold-price-forecast-xau-usd-warsh-dollar-4200/). That is consistent with a smaller near-term rate threat, not a flight to safety.
