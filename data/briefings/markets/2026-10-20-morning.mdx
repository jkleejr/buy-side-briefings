## Chip Stocks' Monday Rebound Is Fading as Seoul and AI Cloud Shares Fall Again

The selling that ended on Monday resumed overnight in the same places it started. Seoul's Kospi fell 3.73%, with SK hynix down 4.23%. In US premarket trading, CoreWeave fell 4.7%, and Nasdaq 100 futures were down about 0.6%, enough to give back roughly half of Monday's 1.11% Nasdaq gain. The decline is narrow: S&P 500 futures were off about 0.15% and Dow futures were up about 0.2%, helped by RTX's results. Oil near $110-111 and a 10-year yield near 5.13% are not helping borrowers who depend on cheap money. Monday's rebound has not held so far. An S&P 500 close back below 7,666.45 would confirm that; a CoreWeave close above Monday's 52-week low would argue against it.

## What changed since last report

Last night's report read Monday as relief rather than an easing of the financing worry, because CoreWeave made a new low while chip stocks rose. That reading has held, with one correction: the 10-year closed Monday nearer [5.15%, up about 4 basis points](https://cnbc.com/2026/10/19/bonds-treasurys-us-yields.html), not the 5.08% given last night, so bonds were working against the rebound rather than staying flat.

- **AI funding, not chip demand.** Elice's weak debut and HSBC's bearish call on CoreWeave are both about who will fund AI capacity and at what cost. Nothing new has been reported about demand for chips.
- **Seoul passed the worry on to memory makers.** The Kospi had already fallen about 21% over seven sessions before Tuesday. SK hynix's 4.23% fall put it on course for its lowest close since April, and in early European trading [ASML fell 2.5%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/19/stock-market-today-live-updates.html).
- **Oil reversed Monday's relief.** Kpler's estimate of Gulf crude flows near 8.5 million barrels a day, against more than 20 million before the war, outweighs the Russia waiver. [FedWatch still prices about a 15% chance of an October hike and 89% for December](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/gold-stalls-below-4-200-as-fed-rate-hike-bets-limit-upside-202610191104).
