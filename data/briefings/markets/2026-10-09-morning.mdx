## Delta Cut Its Full-Year Profit Outlook by About a Quarter on Fuel as Earnings Season Opens

Delta's third-quarter report is the first large-company result of the season and the clearest measure yet of what Brent above $100 is doing to profits. Revenue rose 21% and the company said demand held, but its fuel bill rose 62% and the midpoint of its full-year earnings range fell about 24%. The damage came from cost, not sales: customers are still spending, and margins are absorbing the oil price. That is the pattern to check in next week's bank results and in the CPI report on October 14. Stock futures are higher and oil is 1.3% lower this morning, so the market is not treating one airline as a market-wide signal. If Brent falls back under $100, the pressure Delta described eases for every fuel-heavy company reporting this month.

- [Delta earned $1.72 a share on an adjusted basis against about $1.75 expected, and cut its 2026 range to $5.10-5.60 from $6.50-7.50](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/09/delta-air-lines-dal-q3-2026-earnings.html). Its first earnings miss in two years came from costs while the company said demand remained strong.
- [Fuel cost $4.14 billion in the quarter, up 62%, more than $500 million above July's guidance, and Delta expects a roughly $6 billion increase in its fuel bill for the year](https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/delta-air-lines-cuts-profit-outlook-as-fuel-costs-outpace-fare-gains-4873218). The adjusted operating margin of 9.4% sits below the 11-13% it guided in July, even with fourth-quarter revenue guided up about 20% and its Philadelphia-area refinery expected to earn about $700 million this year as a partial offset.
- [Brent fell 1.3% to $102.91 after President Trump said the US will not attack Iran before the November 3 midterms](https://sundayguardianlive.com/world/crude-oil-prices-today-brent-falls-13-to-10291-wti-drops-12-to-9040-as-trump-signals-no-iran-attack-before-november-3-midterm-elections-302059/). The US blockade of Iranian shipping in Hormuz stays in place and Hurricane Isaias has shut in about 1.28 million barrels a day of Gulf output, which limits how far oil can fall on the pledge.
- [S&P 500 futures rose 0.4% and Nasdaq 100 futures 0.8% by 7:32 a.m. ET, while Dow futures edged up 0.1%](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-october-9-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-080148117.html). The recovery is led by the AI names that fell hardest on Thursday, not by companies exposed to fuel.
- [The ten-year yield is near 5.227%, essentially flat](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/08/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), so this morning's stock gains are not coming from lower rates.
